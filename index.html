ABOUT SANSKRITI

Where Culture Meets
Creativity.

Sanskriti is the official cultural club of Punjab State Aeronautical Engineering College, created to provide students with a platform to express, explore and celebrate their creativity.

From dance, music and theatre to fine arts and literary activities, Sanskriti brings together diverse talents and creates opportunities for students to participate, perform and grow.

We believe that college life is not only about academics. It is also about discovering your passion, building confidence, creating memories and becoming part of something bigger.
